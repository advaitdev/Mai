# Mai (*M*inecraft *A*rtificial *I*ntelligence)

<h3><img align="center" height="30" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9129e5a6-8194-41c3-ac4c-6a93b512304e"> Mai is an LLM-powered Minecraft plugin that drops NPCs into your world to play alongside you. The idea is to make "multiplayer" feel real even when you don't have friends online — or when you'd rather not hand out whitelist access to strangers.</h3>
